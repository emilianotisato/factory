Decided 2026-10-13 during the PRD-002 revision: **this is PRD-004, built after 002.** The owner wants the skills held here as the source, installed into a repo (`.claude/skills`, and the Codex and Cursor equivalents) or the user dir, and updated by `factory` when the framework evolves, in full coordination with the binary. PRD-002 FR-17 edits the 23 `docs.mjs` lines in place first; 004 redoes them as in-repo files.

**Recon for the PRD interview, 2026-10-13, paused by the owner for a content review first.** Three retros (001, 002, 003) each wrote a "Framework friction → skills repo" section, about twelve concrete edits in all; none was applied in the Dropbox repo (the 30-file second-opinion limit flagged twice, `polish` §9 still tells an unattended worker to commit, the retro template still names `measure.py`). The family has no personal paths; the only personal facts are in `feature-docs/models.md`, which is the one case for a user-scope overlay. Siblings link `../feature-docs/models.md` 8 times and `../feature-docs/autonomy.md` 6 times. Lean on install: no choice offered, rendered into the repo and committed by `factory init`; the user scope holds overlays only. **Order: the owner reviews each skill interactively against the retros and the metrics first, edits land in the Dropbox repo, then PRD-004 ports the result.**
